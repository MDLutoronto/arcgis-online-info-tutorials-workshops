# ArcGIS Online: Information, Tutorials, and Workshops

<img src='{{ '/assets/images/arcgis-online-logo.jpg' | relative_url }}' alt="
ArcGIS Online logo" width='100%' height='100%' />

<b><a href="https://utoronto.maps.arcgis.com/home/index.html"
target="_blank">ArcGIS Online</a></b> is a <b>web-based, platform-as-a-service
geographic information systems (GIS) software</b> developed by Esri. It allows you to build
and share <b>interactive web maps
and no-code apps</b>.

Logging in with any web browser using your UTORid will
enable you to access UofT's subscription to the service, which is
administered by the Map and Data Library.

ArcGIS Online a
popular choice for new learners, or as a tool
enabling those who don’t have GIS or web development backgrounds to create
custom, high quality digital products, such as 
<a href="https://mdlutoronto.github.io/tutorials-search/?tool=ArcGIS+Storymaps"
target="_blank">ArcGIS StoryMaps</a>.

You should be aware, however, that <b>ArcGIS Online is not a permanent archive</b> —
inactive accounts and old content may be deleted. Please review our 
<a href="https://mdl.library.utoronto.ca/technology/gis-software/arcgis-online-data-retention-policy"
target="_blank">data retention policy</a> to
understand how your data is managed and how to preserve important work.
Additionally, <b>server resources on the platform are limited</b>. <b>Please review
our <a href="https://mdlutoronto.github.io/arcgis-online-data-management/"
target="_blank">best practices for data management</a> before undertaking project work in
ArcGIS Online.</b>
